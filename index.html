About SDG 6

SDG 6 aims to ensure access to water and sanitation for all. Despite progress, billions of people still lack these basic services. Water scarcity affects more than 40% of the global population and is projected to rise.

Our Mission

Key Focus Areas

Water Access

Ensuring universal access to safe and affordable drinking water for all communities.

Sanitation

Improving access to adequate and equitable sanitation and hygiene facilities.

Water Quality

Protecting and restoring water-related ecosystems for sustainable development.
